Learning progress

The baseline triggers at a lower-bound index of 61. One earliest eligible case per neighborhood per noon-to-noon episode is counted, so repeated reports from one fog event do not dominate.

Candidate thresholds use the earliest 70% of episodes for fitting and the later 30% for evaluation. Candidates are review-only; weights and homepage behavior are never changed here. Sparse or selective reporting can still bias these results.
