TRANSPARENT BY DESIGN

What this first version measures

These are forecast-guidance proxies, not a fully diagnosed atmospheric-pattern model. Each component is calculated for every complete ensemble member, then averaged. At least 10 members and 80% of the available ensemble must be usable.

Cold Pattern

Daily mean temperature maps linearly from 0 at 5°C (41°F) to 100 at −5°C (23°F). This measures absolute cold support, not departure from normal.

Storm Track

Daily precipitation maps from 0 at 0 mm to 100 at 10 mm (0.39 in). This is a local precipitation proxy; storm tracks and jet anomalies are not directly diagnosed.

Snow Setup

Modeled snowfall counts only in hours with precipitation and temperature at or below 2°C. Daily coincident snowfall maps from 0 at 0 cm to 100 at 2.54 cm (1 in).

All scores are bounded 0–100. Summary cards and forecast windows show the strongest daily ensemble score, with its date and confidence—not the chance of an event anywhere in the period. Favorable stretches are consecutive days scoring 60 or higher, using the existing favorable category. Cold and storm peaks may occur on different days; only the snow score measures their modeled overlap. One ensemble system is used. Confidence is capped at Moderate for days 1–5 and Low beyond day 5; broad temperature spread also lowers confidence.

Higher ridges, sheltered valleys and cold-air damming can produce conditions that this forecast grid does not resolve. Consult the National Weather Service for official forecasts and alerts.

Research findings and limitations

The recovered v0.5 study reported +8.47% first-week cold Brier skill versus persistence in 15 exploratory winters. This dashboard uses a different ensemble heuristic; that result does not validate these scores. v0.4 already evaluated the period later labeled a holdout, the v0.5 report misstates the seven-day event thresholds, and the historical station reader ignored quality flags. An independent model refit and operational benchmark remain necessary.

Sources & freshness

Forecast initialization time is not supplied by this API; retrieval time is shown instead. Automatic refresh is scheduled each morning, with cached retrieval during the day. Failed refreshes preserve and label the last successful assessment.
