828 Weather Direct Explainer

FeelScore

FeelScore turns the weather into one quick comfort read. It blends temperature, humidity, dew point, and wind so the number reflects how the air actually feels, not just what the thermometer says.

Right now: 82 - Great

What raises it

Mild temperatures, lower dew points, manageable humidity, and a gentle breeze push the score higher.

What lowers it

Heat, sticky humidity, sharp cold, strong wind, or a raw damp feel pull the score down.

Comfort scale

90-100Ideal: easy air, comfortable temperatures, and very little weather friction.
70-89Comfortable: broadly pleasant, though one factor may be noticeable.
55-69Slight discomfort: usable, but heat, chill, humidity, or wind starts to matter.
40-54Uncomfortable: conditions are working against comfort for many people.
<40Harsh: cold, heat, humidity, wind, or wet weather may dominate the feel.

How to use it

  • Use the score as a quick read on comfort, then scan the bullets to see what is driving it.
  • Higher is not always hotter. A crisp, dry 58 can score better than a muggy 74.
  • Wind can help on warm days, but it can punish the score when the air is chilly or damp.
