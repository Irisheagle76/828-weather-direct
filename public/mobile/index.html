More info · Understanding FEELSCORE

FEELSCORE combines temperature, moisture, wind and weather into a 0–100 comfort score. Higher scores mean greater weather comfort—not a safety rating or a promise that every activity will be suitable.

Each time window shows the lowest and highest available hourly scores, not an average. Windows use Eastern time and end at the stated time: 5–9 AM means 5 AM up to, but not including, 9 AM. Gaps between your four windows are intentional.

Best Window is the strongest continuous two-hour stretch within these windows, ranked by its lowest hourly score. It is a relative best, not necessarily comfortable. Today uses only upcoming hours; elapsed windows are tucked below. Missing guidance is never filled with current conditions.
