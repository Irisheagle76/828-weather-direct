About the weather desk

828 Weather Direct and Tim Ballisty

828 Weather Direct is a hyper-local weather service for Asheville, North Carolina and the surrounding Western North Carolina and southern Blue Ridge region. Forecasts and original analysis are produced by meteorologist Tim Ballisty.

Built around Asheville's terrain

Mountain weather can change over short distances. The site's forecasts and tools add local context for elevation, valleys, ridges, exposure and neighborhood microclimates instead of treating one observation as the whole Asheville story.

What 828 Weather Direct covers

The site brings together current conditions, a four-day forecast, Weather Pulse updates, radar and satellite imagery, rainfall and water guidance, hiking weather, fall color guidance, mountain cameras, FeelScore and Sunset Radiance.

Sources and transparency

Pages identify their relevant sources, including NOAA and the National Weather Service, Tempest, Open-Meteo, local station networks and camera observations. Local tools interpret those inputs for practical planning.

Official weather information

For watches, warnings and emergency guidance, always use NOAA, the National Weather Service and the appropriate local authorities. 828 Weather Direct provides local analysis and general awareness, not an official warning service.
