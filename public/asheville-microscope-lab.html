Map guide

How to read this weather map

This interactive map places current observations in their real geographic context. It shows what is happening now—not a forecast.

1
One dot equals one weather stationIts position is based on the station’s approximate latitude and longitude.
2
Use the map for geographic contextThe quiet gray basemap preserves roads, waterways and terrain while deliberately removing most labels. Pan or zoom when you need more detail.
3
Color answers “lower or higher?”Blue dots are toward the lower end of the selected weather layer; orange dots are toward the higher end. The exact reading appears when you select a dot.

Important: A different color does not automatically mean a faulty station. Elevation, shade, rain, wind exposure and neighborhood setting can all create real local differences.
