Good, watch sun
Hiker read

Forest shade is the friendliest choice; open ridges and pavement will feel warmer.

If you are heading out, Asheville-area trails look good, watch sun. The nearby readings sit within about 5°F of each other, so elevation alone may not change the feel very much. The two high Asheville readings are telling a similar ridge story, which means shade, slope, and open exposure still matter. Mount Mitchell is about 14°F cooler than the Asheville-area readings, so high-peak hikes deserve an extra layer and a separate comfort check. Fog is not the main signal right now. Open routes need sun awareness and steady water; forested trails should feel more forgiving.
