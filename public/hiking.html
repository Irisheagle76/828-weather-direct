Good, watch sun
Hiker read

Forest shade is the friendliest choice; open ridges and pavement will feel warmer.

If you are heading out, Asheville-area trails look good, watch sun. The nearby readings sit within about 7°F of each other, so elevation alone may not change the feel very much. The two high Asheville readings differ enough to show local microclimates at work, which means shade, slope, and open exposure still matter. Mount Mitchell is about 20°F cooler than the Asheville-area readings, so high-peak hikes deserve an extra layer and a separate comfort check. Fog is not the main signal right now. Open routes need sun awareness and steady water; forested trails should feel more forgiving.
