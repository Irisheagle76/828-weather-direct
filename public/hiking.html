If you are heading out, Asheville-area trails look good for most hikes. The nearby readings sit within about 8°F of each other, so elevation alone may not change the feel very much. Mount Mitchell is about 16°F cooler than the Asheville-area readings, so high-peak hikes deserve an extra layer and a separate comfort check. Fog is not the main signal right now. Open routes need sun awareness and steady water; forested trails should feel more forgiving.
