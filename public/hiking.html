Good for most hikes
Hiker read

Most nearby trails look friendly, with normal mountain checks.

If you are heading out, Asheville-area trails look good for most hikes. The nearby readings sit within about 7°F of each other, so elevation alone may not change the feel very much. The two high Asheville readings are telling a similar ridge story, which means shade, slope, and open exposure still matter. Mount Mitchell is about 17°F cooler than the Asheville-area readings, so high-peak hikes deserve an extra layer and a separate comfort check. Fog is not the main signal right now. Sun exposure is manageable, especially under canopy.
