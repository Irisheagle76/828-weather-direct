Good for most hikes
Hiker read

Good overall, but expect damp pockets, wet leaves, or low cloud early.

If you are heading out, Asheville-area trails look good for most hikes. The nearby readings sit within about 7°F of each other, so elevation alone may not change the feel very much. The two high Asheville readings are telling a similar ridge story, which means shade, slope, and open exposure still matter. Mount Mitchell is about 15°F cooler than the Asheville-area readings, so high-peak hikes deserve an extra layer and a separate comfort check. Moisture is close enough for fog, low cloud, or wet leaves in sheltered spots. Sun exposure is manageable, especially under canopy.
