Why this matters for fall color +

Repeated cool nights are part of the seasonal transition that accompanies fall color progression. Elevation strongly influences how quickly these colder conditions arrive across Western North Carolina.

Cool-night and frost signals describe color-development weather. Hard freezes, heavy rain and strong wind are tracked separately as leaf-drop weather because they can eventually increase damage and leaf loss.
