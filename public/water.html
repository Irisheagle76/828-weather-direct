How this index is calculated Tap for score breakdown
Rain drives the score

Recent radar rain is sampled throughout the full upstream watershed, then combined with antecedent rainfall to estimate how full each waterfall should look.

Basin rain, not one map point

The recent signal is 85% basin-wide average and 15% wettest-quartile rain, so a localized mountain storm matters without one radar cell taking over.

Each basin responds differently

Fast-response creeks react quickly to storms. Larger or slower basins hold water longer, so older rain still matters.

Safety modifies the read

High recent rain can boost the hazard signal for waterfalls with slick-rock or high-current sensitivity.

Scores are guidance, not guarantees

Radar and model estimates can miss localized downpours. Confirm cameras, closures, trail conditions, and your own comfort level.
