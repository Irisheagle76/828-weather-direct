# September 2026 Fall Explorer input

The versioned lib/fall/seasonal-inputs.json records Howard Neufeld's completed September 2026 monthly summary of 66.6°F from his backyard Ambient Weather station in Vilas. His October 3 report is linked in the artifact. The project owner confirmed on October 5 that this station and high/low averaging method supplied the historical research series.

This is author-reported, not an independent recomputation. Daily records, station ID, quality flags, and observation-day convention were not supplied; the artifact explicitly preserves those unknowns. Do not invent daily records or claim independent QC.

The departure from 64.7°F is +1.9°F, producing +3.61285 days in the published regression. The page rounds the midpoint to October 20 and displays its existing October 17–23 planning window. The window is not a confidence interval and the model applies only to Boone–Grandfather at 2,500–4,000 feet. The author's separate assessment of roughly one week late includes current weather and foliage observations and is not the regression result.

The API selects this reviewed year-specific artifact after September ends in Eastern time, ahead of legacy environment values. Other years retain null unless a valid year-tagged legacy input is provided. No production environment edit is required. Review new yearly artifacts and their provenance before adding them. Missing inputs remain historical baseline estimates.
