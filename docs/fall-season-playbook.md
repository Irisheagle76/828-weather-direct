# Fall Explorer seasonal playbook

Recorded October 25, 2026 at the owner's request. Start here when preparing September and October 2027. This is repository memory, not a scheduled reminder. Implementation in the working checkout does not establish production release status.

## What we learned and built in 2026

- Keep the page useful for decisions: today's weather, where and when to go, remaining-color weather consequences, cameras, then supporting science and seasonal history.
- A weather score is not a foliage score. Separate seasonally plausible places to check from best mountain-view weather. Starting mid-October, emphasize lower/middle elevations and show lower cameras first; never label those destinations confirmed peak from a calendar.
- The Boone–Grandfather regression is limited to 2,500–4,000 ft. Baseline September mean is 64.7°F. Do not extrapolate its midpoint across WNC or interpret it as season end. Collapse the estimate into seasonal context; use past tense after its three-day planning-window margin passes in Eastern time.
- September input provenance remains unresolved in the recorded investigation. NOAA Boone and Boone airport historical values did not match the original calibration anchors. Do not silently substitute either series, impute missing September days, or describe the baseline as a measured current-year prediction.
- Weather consequence analysis combines cold duration/sequence, wind, rain and sunlight with estimated stage. Stage and drought limitations keep confidence low. First freeze alone cannot prove damaged foliage or canopy loss. No owner reporting workflow is required.
- Make camera freshness honest: image load time does not establish capture time. Link to source timestamps; unknown capture age must remain explicit. A successfully downloaded image can still be stale.
- Show the next forecast leaf-drop concern and a favorable preceding day when supported. Identify its geographic scope. Do not issue unsupported last-chance countdowns.
- Shift November editorial emphasis toward views, photography and hiking without declaring regional season end from date or freeze alone. A confirmed end-of-season recap requires dated canopy evidence.

## Consumer-language lesson from October 25 review

The owner found repeated “Color Retention Favored / Low confidence” cards unhelpful. Public cards should answer: what may happen to leaves, what that means for this destination, what to do before traveling, and which dated forecast numbers support the advice. Distinguish town/lower-slope color searches from high-ridge scenery. Put shared uncertainty in one clear note; preserve technical categories in the expandable method section. Test missing forecasts and partial wind/rain coverage so numbers do not imply evidence we lack.
